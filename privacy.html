Clipping OS — Privacy Policy Effective date: 29 September 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Clipping OS handles information when you use the local-first application. 1. Information handled Depending on the features you use, Clipping OS may handle: connected-provider account identifiers and capability information; OAuth access and refresh credentials, stored in the computer's secure credential storage; source video files, transcripts, candidate clips, captions, hooks and render metadata; posting captions, hashtags, destinations, times, upload identifiers and audit events; local quality-control results, hashes and review decisions; diagnostic information needed to identify failures. 2. Where information is stored Clipping OS is designed to keep project media, transcripts, renders, reviews and audit records on your computer. OAuth credentials are intended to remain in the operating system's secure credential store rather than in the project repository, logs or ordinary configuration files. 3. Third-party providers If you connect an account or explicitly use a provider feature, information necessary for that action may be sent to the selected provider, such as YouTube or TikTok, through its official API. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Clipping OS does not receive your platform password. 4. Optional AI analysis If you enable an AI-assisted feature, selected transcript text, candidate information or sampled media frames may be sent to the configured AI provider for analysis. Do not send personal, confidential or third-party material unless you have permission to do so. Credentials, cookies and signed URLs must not be included in AI requests. 5. Sharing and sale Clipping OS does not sell personal information. The local application is not designed to share project media or account credentials with the project maintainer. Provider API calls and optional AI requests occur only when the relevant feature is used and configured. 6. Retention and deletion Local files and records remain on your computer until you delete them. You can disconnect a provider and remove its local credential through the available controls. Deleting a local credential does not necessarily revoke access already granted to a third-party provider; use that provider's account settings when provider-side revocation is required. 7. Security Clipping OS uses approval gates, redacted logs and secure credential-storage hooks. No software can guarantee absolute security. Keep your operating system, repository access and connected accounts protected. 8. Children Clipping OS is not directed to children. Do not use it to process a child's personal information without appropriate permission and safeguards. 9. Changes This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is made. 10. Contact Questions or privacy requests can be raised through the public repository issue tracker: GitHub Issues.
